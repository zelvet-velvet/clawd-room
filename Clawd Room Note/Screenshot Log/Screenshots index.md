# 📷 Screenshots

The pictures of clawd-room: what the room looked like, mockups that were picked from, the user's own screenshots. **`Screenshots/` is the source**: one folder per subject with the picture files (`YYYY-MM-DD subject.png`; a person's own: `... (Zaviel).png`; the picked mockup: `... (picked).png`). Browse them in the notes below (this folder, Screenshot Log, written by `tools/vault_gallery.py`); the [[Dev Log]] shows them with what changed.

> [!tip] Adding a picture
> 1. Save it in the subject folder that fits, named `YYYY-MM-DD subject.png` (names stay unique across folders).
> 2. Embed it in its [[Dev Log]] entry: `![[YYYY-MM-DD subject.png|300]]`.
> 3. `python3 tools/vault_gallery.py` rewrites the notes in Screenshot Log (CMD_SYNC runs it too).
