## 🔦 The picked Clawd stands out (Zaviel)
> [!info] Branch `feat/picked-clawd` (#6) · task W4
- Press **Tab** (or `A` / `D` while following) and four yellow corners close in around the Clawd the camera is on, while the rest of the room dims by 60 in 100 around a soft spotlight. It fades in over 0.25 s.
- Press Tab again and the corners **glide** to the next Clawd (0.2 s); 2.5 s after the last press they fade out (0.4 s). If the Clawd leaves, they fade at once.
- Zoomed in, the light and corners scale with the room and follow the Clawd as it walks.
- The highlight is a **circle** (radius 20 room pixels), not an oval.
- **Esc** lets go of the picked Clawd (the corners fade out at once); it **no longer quits** the room: only `q` does. README, Usage, Interaction and the LLM Guide say so.
- **Centred:** a zoomed camera in auto follow or on a picked Clawd keeps that Clawd in the middle of the view all the time; it no longer waits until the Clawd nears the edge (the old dead zone is gone). To look at by eye: T62 in [[Test Checklist]].
- New scene `office-pick-clawd` (`key T tab SID` picks a Clawd in a scenario); to look at by eye: T58 – T61 in [[Test Checklist]]. Design: [[Interaction]].
