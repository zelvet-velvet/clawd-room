## 📐 Rooms in tall panes keep a normal floor, and panes of one window share a scale (Zaviel)
- In a pane taller than ¾ of its width the room is drawn at most ¾ as tall as wide, in the middle of the pane with dark above and below; before, the floor stretched into a long runway.
- Rooms in panes of the same window use one scale (the smallest of their own), so a room with few Clawds no longer looks twice as big as its neighbour.
- Picked from four options (D); whole windows are unchanged. Branch `feat/split-panes` (#15); [[Platforms and Windows]] (Split panes).

![[2026-11-01 room height in split panes, options.png|774]]

## 🚪 A gate between two rooms opens at the same moment on both sides (Zaviel)
- Seen on WSL with two room panes: one room's gate rose up to 1.5 s before the other's, as each room learns the other's pane from its heartbeat.
- Now each room says in its heartbeat when it was ready to show the gate, and both start at the later of the two plus 1 s. A gate shows about a second later than before, but on both sides together; with a room that does not say (an older version) it starts on its own after 3 s.
- Branch `feat/split-panes` (#15); [[Interaction]], [[Platforms and Windows]].

## 🪟 Two room panes opened quickly both find their pane (Zaviel)
- Seen on WSL: of two Clawd Room panes opened one after the other, the first never learned which pane was its own, so it got no gate, and after 5 s it resized the window and changed the font as if it had a window to itself.
- Now a room whose pane lost the focus too early takes the one Clawd Room pane the other rooms do not claim, in any split window, and gets its gates.
- A room that cannot tell within 15 s whether it is in a pane leaves the window and the font alone; nor does it reset the font on exit once its window has been split.
- Branch `feat/split-panes` (#15); [[Platforms and Windows]] (Split panes).

## 🦀 A pane too small for the room shows a strip of Clawds (Zaviel)
- Under 44x20 cells the pane used to go black ("need 44x20" in a tiny font). Now it shows a band of the room with a Clawd at a laptop per session, each screen in the session's state colour; under 4 rows, faces on tiles in the state's colour.
- Tab picks a Clawd as in the room (corners, the rest dimmed) and shows its name. When not all fit, the band follows the picked Clawd, and one out of view that starts waiting is brought in by itself.
- Picked from mockups (E band + I faces, then E with Tab). Branch `feat/split-panes` (#15); [[Rooms and Art]] (Layout).

## 🦀 The strip joins the split-pane branch; its focus frames only the Clawd; no gates to a strip (Zaviel)
- `feat/small-pane-strip` (#18) merged into `feat/split-panes` (#15), so one branch tests both.
- Seen on WSL: in a strip taller than 5 rows Tab framed the whole band's height. Now the corners are the room's frame around the Clawd only, with a round light on it and the name beside the corners.
- A strip has no floor to walk onto, so it says `strip` in its heartbeat and the rooms beside it make no gate to it.
- Open: a room pane beside a strip showed an empty office with "+1 MORE" (one session). Geometry seats one Clawd at every size, so the room counted a Clawd it did not draw; not reproduced yet. [[Rooms and Art]] (Layout), [[Platforms and Windows]].

## 🦀 A room with no seat for one Clawd shows the strip; the strip's name goes above, sleeping Clawds get Zs (Zaviel)
- Seen on WSL: an empty office saying "+1 MORE" with one session. Cause: a room under about 24 rows (the park under about 40) has no seat for even one Clawd. Picked: show the strip then (`strip_no_seat`), not an empty floor. A room that seats some but not all still says "+N MORE" until the crowded rooms are built.
- The strip's name sits above the corners when the band is tall enough, not over the Clawd. A sleeping Clawd shuts its eyes and gets the room's Zs; an idle one keeps its eyes open.
- Clawds too big in a pane: the room does not change a pane's font; Ctrl+- in that pane makes them smaller (picked, no code). [[Rooms and Art]] (Layout), [[Platforms and Windows]], `docs/Usage.md`.
