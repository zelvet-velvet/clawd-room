## 📐 Rooms in tall panes keep a normal floor, and panes of one window share a scale (Zaviel)
- In a pane taller than ¾ of its width the room is drawn at most ¾ as tall as wide, in the middle of the pane with dark above and below; before, the floor stretched into a long runway.
- Rooms in panes of the same window use one scale (the smallest of their own), so a room with few Clawds no longer looks twice as big as its neighbour.
- Picked from four options (D); whole windows are unchanged. Branch `feat/split-panes` (#15); [[Platforms and Windows]] (Split panes).

## 🚪 A gate between two rooms opens at the same moment on both sides (Zaviel)
- Seen on WSL with two room panes: one room's gate rose up to 1.5 s before the other's, as each room learns the other's pane from its heartbeat.
- Now each room says in its heartbeat when it was ready to show the gate, and both start at the later of the two plus 1 s. A gate shows about a second later than before, but on both sides together; with a room that does not say (an older version) it starts on its own after 3 s.
- Branch `feat/split-panes` (#15); [[Interaction]], [[Platforms and Windows]].

## 🪟 Two room panes opened quickly both find their pane (Zaviel)
- Seen on WSL: of two Clawd Room panes opened one after the other, the first never learned which pane was its own, so it got no gate, and after 5 s it resized the window and changed the font as if it had a window to itself.
- Now a room whose pane lost the focus too early takes the one Clawd Room pane the other rooms do not claim, in any split window, and gets its gates.
- A room that cannot tell within 15 s whether it is in a pane leaves the window and the font alone; nor does it reset the font on exit once its window has been split.
- Branch `feat/split-panes` (#15); [[Platforms and Windows]] (Split panes).
